About Me

Hi! I am a Game Design student and digital artist with a lifelong passion for visual storytelling.

I started with traditional drawing and gradually transitioned into digital art, exploring character design, illustration, and animation along the way.

While I study game design, I naturally gravitate toward the visual and artistic side of development, especially character design, aesthetics, and world-building. I enjoy shaping the look and feel of interactive experiences and bringing ideas to life visually. I am experienced with tools like Adobe Suite, Krita, Procreate, Aseprite, Pixelorama and other softwares.
